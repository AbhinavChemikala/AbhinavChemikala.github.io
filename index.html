About Me

I'm a Game Developer with 3 years of professional experience building and shipping mobile games that kids actually love. Based in Hyderabad, India, I work at Tigris Entertainment — sister company of Green Gold Animation, the studio behind Chhota Bheem.

My stack is Unity + C# for mobile, with Unreal Engine in my academic toolkit. I've shipped 4+ titles on Google Play and the App Store and built HTML5 games for clients like Jio Games and Glance.

I actively use AI tools like GitHub Copilot, OpenAI Codex, and AI Agents to automate repetitive dev work and move faster without cutting corners. Outside of work I build production-grade side projects — currently SpendWise, an AI-powered Android finance tracker.
